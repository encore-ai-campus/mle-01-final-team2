4. VALUES

Do not invent missing operators. EQ/NEQ use scalars, IN/NOT_IN nonempty explicit alternatives. BETWEEN uses {lower:{value,inclusive},upper:{value,inclusive}}; use both original bounds and explicit source boundary meanings. Never default inclusivity. No arbitrary object values, minimum/maximum dictionaries, or comma splitting.

5. TYPES
Independently inspect ELIGIBILITY, EXCLUSION and PREFERENCE. A preference is not a mandatory requirement. An exclusion is not positive eligibility. Do not invent absent exclusion/preference content. A future obligation is not a current fact.

6. ATOMS
One atom per independent entity/role, predicate, comparator, value, scope and time. Separate subject facts from document obligations; separate applicant/representative/site/team roles. Preserve ALL/ANY quantification; unsupported quantifiers or concepts require review. Do not store multiple requirements in a long value. Splitting atoms must retain the original AND/OR and exception scope.

7. QUOTES

The first quote is primary; keep it uniquely locatable and sufficiently complete.

8. LOGIC
Use explicit logic_groups with stable group_id and ordered child refs; logic_sections identifies top-level root_ref. CONDITION_REF indexes conditions; UNRESOLVED_REF indexes unresolved_conditions; GROUP_REF names an existing group. AND/OR require at least two children, NOT exactly one; UNRESOLVED at least one. Nested groups only if supported by source. Keep separate flat roots when nesting is not stated. No global AND default. Do not reference nonexistent IDs or create cycles/orphans/duplicate groups. Exceptions preserve target_refs, effect, scope and precondition; unresolved targets block application. unresolved_relations preserve original member refs and reason. Mark INCOMPLETE when conditions/targets are unresolved; never silently remove rejected refs or promote to complete.

9. UNCERTAINTY
Distinguish no discovery, not stated, unclear interpretation, unsupported attribute, explicit no restriction and review required. Missing source is not confirmed absence. Preserve raw_condition/evidence/proposed_field/entity/type/reason/review status for unresolved entries. No unsupported concept becomes an approved canonical attribute. Always decision_eligible:false; proposals remain unreviewed.
